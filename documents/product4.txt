**Mystery Book**:

A blank hardcover book with pages that slowly fill themselves based on the reader’s daily experiences.
Uses heat-sensitive ink to record thoughts, conversations, and events when the book is nearby.
No batteries or setup required.
The writing appears in the reader’s handwriting style and fades after 24 hours unless marked as "permanent" by folding a page corner.
The cover is waterproof, and the spine adjusts to expand or shrink as needed.
No instructions included—just open it and let it document life as it happens.
