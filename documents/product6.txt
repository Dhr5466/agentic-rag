**TimberRide**:

A bicycle frame handcrafted from layered bamboo and ash wood, treated with a waterproof resin for durability.
The wood absorbs vibrations better than metal, making rides smoother.
Equipped with a carbon fiber fork and lightweight aluminum components for balance.
Single-speed or optional 3-speed internal hub. Weighs 12 kg.
The frame flexes slightly for comfort but stays rigid for efficiency.
Requires occasional oiling to maintain the wood.

Available in natural finish or stained options.

Not for extreme weather or off-road use.
Minimalist design, built to last.
