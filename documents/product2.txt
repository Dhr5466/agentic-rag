**SmartBrew Mini**:

A compact, app-controlled coffee maker for single-serve brewing.
Uses Wi-Fi to sync with your phone, so you can schedule brews, adjust strength, and monitor water levels remotely.
Compatible with pods or ground coffee, with a 12-ounce capacity.
Features a self-cleaning function and energy-saving mode.
Voice assistant support for hands-free operation.
Stainless steel design, fits on small countertops.
Brews in under 90 seconds.
No complicated settings—just fresh coffee, your way, on demand.
